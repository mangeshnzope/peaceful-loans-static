Critical Checklist: Things to Watch

Insurance Bundling Pushes

Private lenders like HDFC and ICICI often make low interest rates conditional upon buying expensive home loan protection plans (~₹3-4L). These are 100% optional by RBI mandate. We will ensure this is kept completely unbundled or skipped entirely.

MOTD Registration Timing

Government banks (Canara, SBI) defer the 0.2% MOTD charge until the final property registration stage near possession. Private banks force you to pay this 0.2% upfront at first disbursement, draining cash flow in early construction phases.

Top-Up Interiors Loan

Some banks permit you to club an interiors styling/renovation top-up loan alongside your main home loan once the structure is fully completed. Let us know if you require this top-up so we can lock in matching approvals.

Rate Movements (Floating Rates)

All home loan rates are floating and pegged to the Repo Rate (EBLR/RLLR). If the RBI cuts the base repo rate, your floating interest rates will fall in lockstep automatically within 3 months, irrespective of the bank you choose.
