Strategic Thesis

Peaceful Loans has successfully established product-market fit. Over the past three financial years, the business has moved from proof-of-concept (FY24) to rapid validation (FY25), followed by sustainable, non-dilutive scaling (FY26).

Our structural pivot toward high-ticket Home Loans (HL) from Education Loans (EL) changes our economics, driving higher average transaction sizes (ATS) and invoicing values. Year 4 projections are firmly grounded in this operational shift.

HL Funnel — Corrected View

The true story of the funnel after cleansing Zoho data of intentional relegations and auto-declined tickets.

The 72% "drop-off" is not a leaky funnel problem.

It's actually a deliberate quality filter — with a very small actual loss layer underneath.

Zoho timeline checks reveal: Sep-25 bulk import contained 39 leads immediately marked "Not Live" within 3 days. From April-26, website forms auto-flag and filter out sub-2Cr enquiries (11 leads). Real operational losses are limited to 11 leads.

Investor Narrative Impact & The Core Fix

Reframed Funnel health: The corrected conversion rate is 16.9%. This represents a highly healthy DSA conversion rate for Home Loans (the industry standard floats around 10-15%).

The Core operational fix: To prevent losses in the late processing stages (e.g. ASHISH BHAYANA category), we are implementing a mandatory progress call checklist within 48 hours of all logins being completed.
