The Handwritten Letter From Mangesh Zope

Every teammate received this handwritten letter prior to our Google Meet / Zoom session. Unfold it below to revisit the story of Aai’s Airbnb hospitality, the 97.5% benchmark, and the legacy we are building together.

Dear Mrs. Harnehmat Kaur & Every Builder of Peaceful-Loans,

Thank you for being a part of building Peaceful-Loans. Peaceful-Loans is not only a dream, it is also a pride of everyone who is laying every single brick of this “Naa Bhuto, Na Bhavishyati” organisation.

As Shah Rukh Khan said in an interview with Anupam Kher when asked how he would feel if someone else got the limelight and he faded into oblivion, he replied with a single line:

“Yeh ho nahi sakta. Mein sach bol doon… I am the last of the stars.”

Every single person contributing to this mission should keep this in mind: when we are done building, we will be the last of the stars. This will be our legacy.

This thought of making an organization impossible to replicate comes from what I observed from my mother. She has always defined unreasonable hospitality for me. You should see her reviews on Airbnb to know why I am saying so. She has been my coach, mentor and inspiration all my life.

I studied in a Marathi medium school as my parents were uneducated and wanted to guide me in the journey. I never stood second in my entire school life. Second rank in my school was about 82–83% and I stood first with 97.5%. As a 3-year-old kid studying day in, day out, I didn’t know why I was doing it.

Mom, whom I call Aai, kept telling me every day: “If you can see someone in the rear-view mirror, that means you are not working hard enough. Competition should just mentally give up looking at your Herculean effort, intensity and audacity.”

My beloved wife (whom I dragged through this difficult journey of entrepreneurship) wasn’t really aware of what it entails. But she has been a daily source of inspiration on “Grit”. Come rain or shine, her tireless quest of putting in hard yards every single day keeps me silently motivated to keep trying harder and never give up. The pride she feels in Peaceful-Loans’ team delivering such incredible customer service is the true reward which keeps me going.

I am sure each of your family members feels the same way when they see the output of your hard work and the praise showered by customers on you. Enjoy those small moments, you have earned it. You are special! Ask Komal how it feels if you ever get a one-on-one with her beyond just work and tasks.

That is the same intensity with which we are building Peaceful-Loans. I have done my part by putting in as much effort as I can to find a diamond in the dust (by the way, that diamond is You).

A diamond only sparkles after high-intensity cutting. But a diamond also needs inner desire to showcase their A-game. I believe in you. I believe I have hand-picked you as you have the potential to make it. Do you have it in you to work as hard and make it happen?

Half-assing something is such a waste of time.

This brings me to a point: “A compass was invented before a watch, because the direction is more important than speed in this journey.” This is my job to help you understand what we are building here. Provide that direction to our true north. This is my shared dream which I want to communicate through this letter.

We are building a company which stands on these three pillars:
1. Unreasonable Hospitality
2. Unbiased Advice
3. Unmatched Depth of Knowledge

This sounds like an awful lot of effort. Hell yes. It will require a crazy amount of effort. But if you have it in you to compete like Nadal, stamina like Djokovic and flair like Federer, then you are meant for this journey of becoming a legend who will write history alongside me in the Indian Banking Industry.

I sourced your answers regarding what you think Peaceful-Loans does to ensure success. I can bridge the understanding gap. I would love to take this occasion to release our manifesto for all of you.

I strongly recommend for you to watch the webseries on “Made in India — A Titan Story” to visualise how it feels once you get to the end of this journey.

Welcome to the rocketship,
Mangesh Zope
Kabhi Employee, Kabhi Shareholder, But always an ace competitor.
