## Quotes & Highlights from Mangesh Zope in Mint

> **"Bank of Baroda raised its rates after the RBI’s announcement but cut them by 5bps from 10 October, said Mangesh Zope, founder of Peaceful Loans."**
> 
> *In response to Mint’s questions, the bank said its festive campaign offers home loans starting at 7.40% a year, quick sanctions and a full processing-fee waiver until 31 October. Women get an additional 5bps discount, while eligible borrowers under 40, salary-account holders, qualifying high-net-worth individuals and Baroda Radiance customers get 10bps off.*

> **"Zope said processing-fee waivers are useful for affordable housing loans. Bigger home-loan borrowers often seek full or partial refunds from banks or cashback from agents, even outside the festive season. A fee waiver lowers upfront costs, but a slightly lower interest rate could deliver greater long-term savings..."**
