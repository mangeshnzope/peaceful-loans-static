## Key Takeaways from Mangesh Zope in Mint

> **"Never let a headline 'Zero Processing Fee' banner distract you from the interest rate spread."**
> 
> During the festive season, banks launch high-decibel promotional campaigns advertising zero processing fees, temporary rate cuts, and special perks. But for high-value home loan borrowers (>₹1–2 Crore), a processing fee waiver is a one-time saving of ₹10,000 to ₹25,000 (often capped by public sector banks).
> 
> In contrast, securing even a **10 basis points (0.10%) lower spread** on a ₹2 Crore home loan saves over **₹3,03,000** in interest across a 20-year repayment schedule. That is more than **12 times** the value of a waived processing fee.

---

## Bank of Baroda Festive Pricing Moves

Following the Reserve Bank of India's (RBI) 25 bps repo rate hike to 5.50%, banks initially adjusted benchmark rates upwards. However, to maintain retail credit momentum, select lenders introduced festive concessions:

* **Bank of Baroda Adjustment:** As noted by Mangesh Zope, Bank of Baroda initially raised its lending rates following the repo hike, but subsequently lowered its home loan rates by 5 basis points (effective 10 October) for its festive campaign.
* **Festive Terms:** Rates starting at 7.40% per annum, full processing fee waivers, and extra 5–10 bps concessions for women and select salary/HNW client categories.
