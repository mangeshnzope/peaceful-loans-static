Peaceful Loans founder **Mangesh Zope** has been honored in the 10th edition of **BW Disrupt 40 Under 40** by *BW Businessworld* for pioneering borrower-first home loan advisory and mortgage transparency in India.

---

### Who is the Award Given To?
The BW Disrupt 40 Under 40 honours high-impact entrepreneurs, innovators, and intrapreneurs under 40 who are building disruptive ventures, developing game-changing business models, and reshaping established industries through strategy, execution, and technological innovation.

### Why Peaceful Loans Won
Peaceful Loans was created to eliminate the conflict of interest and asymmetric information in Indian mortgage borrowing. Operating on a 100% borrower-first advocacy model, Peaceful Loans acts as an independent fiduciary for home buyers—auditing sanction letters, comparing multi-bank offerings, and negotiating optimal terms without selling borrower data or forcing high-margin products.
