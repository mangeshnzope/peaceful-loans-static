# Compare Bank Interest Rates

Compare current FY26-27 home loan interest rates, spreads, and benchmark repo rates across major Indian banks.

## Benchmarks
In India, residential floating rate home loans are tied to the RBI Repo Rate (External Benchmark Lending Rate / EBLR):
- Effective Rate = Repo Rate + Bank Spread
- Bank Spreads typically range from 1.85% to 2.40% depending on borrower CIBIL score (750+) and loan amount (₹2 Crore+).

## Banks Tracked
- State Bank of India (SBI EBLR)
- HDFC Bank (HDFC Repo Linked Lending Rate)
- ICICI Bank (ICICI Repo Rate Linked Benchmark)
- Bank of Baroda (Baroda BRLLR)
- Kotak Mahindra Bank
- Axis Bank
