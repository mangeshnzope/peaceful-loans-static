# Home Loan Advisory Checklist

Comprehensive audit checklist for ₹2Cr+ home loan sanctions, processing fee waivers, and bank spread negotiations.

## Key Checklist Items
1. **Spread Protection:** Confirm spread is explicitly stated in the sanction letter and cannot be arbitrarily increased by the bank.
2. **Insurance Mandate Elimination:** Prevent illegal bundling of single-premium property or life insurance into the loan amount.
3. **Foreclosure & Prepayment:** Confirm 0% prepayment penalty per RBI guidelines on individual floating-rate loans.
4. **Processing Fee Capping:** Negotiate fee waiver or capped ceiling (typically ₹10,000 to ₹25,000 max for high-value loans).
5. **MODTD / Franking Clarity:** Verify actual state stamp duty on loan documents without bank markup.
