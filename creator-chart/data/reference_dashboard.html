Is our content reaching our target audience?

Two numbers, everywhere. TG impressions: views from people whose LinkedIn seniority is Manager, Director, VP, Owner, CXO or Partner, the closest match to ₹50L+ households taking ₹2 Cr+ home loans. TG share: the % of all views that came from them. A rising share means the content speaks to our TG and LinkedIn is pushing it to them. Creator onboarded 1 Aug; first Creator Chart Era post 19 Aug.

What works: TG share, TG impressions and out-of-network reach

Statistical read of all 54 posts of 2026, each from its own post export. Medians are used because a few viral posts would distort averages. p = chance the gap between groups is just noise (Kruskal–Wallis test for groups, Spearman for relationships). Under 0.05 = reliable, 0.05–0.15 = a lean, above = not proven. With 5–12 posts per group, treat leans as hypotheses to test, not rules.

By type of post

Categories were assigned from each post's opening line. Bank & industry critique = calling out lender practices, RBI/IRDAI moves, bank ads. Home-loan explainer = how rates, EMIs, eligibility work. Client story = a real client or consumer conversation. Founder journey & milestones = Peaceful-Loans' story, wins, numbers. Opinion & life lessons = money/behaviour philosophy. Hiring & team = job posts and team shout-outs.
