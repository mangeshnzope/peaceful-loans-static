## What this is

A suite of 6 interconnected reports presented in a single navigable file with a top tab bar. Each report is a standalone page. Together they tell the full Q1 FY27 story — revenue, funnel health, pipeline, and Q2 targets.

### Report 6 — Funnel by Ticket Size

**Purpose:** Single-page analysis slide showing conversion rates differ significantly by loan ticket size. Key finding: the stall is builder documents, not customer complexity.

**Layout:** 3-column slide with left panel (conversion charts), middle panel (active pipeline by band), right dark panel (key finding).

**Left panel — Stage conversion by ticket band:**

Three bands, each with 3 horizontal bar rows:

*2Cr+ (18 onboarded, 5 disbursed):*
- OB → Processing: 94%
- Processing → Sanctioned: 65%
- **Sanctioned → Disbursed: 45% ⚑** (highlighted in red — this is the problem)

*1–2Cr (24 onboarded, 17 disbursed):*
- OB → Processing: 92%
- Processing → Sanctioned: 95%
- Sanctioned → Disbursed: 81%

*Sub-1Cr (9 onboarded, 7 disbursed):*
- OB → Processing: 100%
- Processing → Sanctioned: 89%
- Sanctioned → Disbursed: 88%

**E2E summary boxes:**
- 2Cr+ E2E: **28%** (5 of 18)
- 1–2Cr E2E: **71%** (17 of 24)
- Sub-1Cr E2E: **78%** (7 of 9)

**Middle panel — Active pipeline blockers:**

*2Cr+ band (13 active · Rs.28.6Cr):*

- 🟡 Samish Bedi 2.60Cr — BOB project approval (green certificate) pending
- 🔵 4× fresh June entries (Sourab, Prashanth, Aakash, Sandeep) 9.90Cr — ATS submitted 20-29 Jun, normal cycle

*1–2Cr band (7 active · Rs.10.0Cr):*
- 🟢 Sandeep Mahapatra + Manoj Kumar 3.04Cr — CMO approval at BOB. Imminent.
- 🟡 Sagar Lohiya 1.96Cr — Sanctioned, waiting seller documents (resale)

**Right panel — Key finding (dark background):**

Headline: *"The stall is not complexity. It's builder documents — and it appears in both bands."*

2 KPIs:

5 findings:
1. 🏗 Builder TPA/NOC delays are the single most common post-sanction blocker. Prestige, Assetz, Sobha all appear in stalled list.

5. 💰 Active 2Cr+ pipeline = Rs.28.6Cr loan value. At current rates: ~Rs.22–25L potential revenue.
